ANUSHA BIRTHDAY V41 — ONLINE SYNC SETUP (English)

WHAT SYNCS ONLINE (after setup)
  Owner -> Visitor (database-driven, no re-publish needed):
    * Chapter 13 gallery: photos, videos, titles, captions, dates, message cards, order, removals
    * Owner voice message (plays at the very bottom of the site)
    * Website song (file or https link)
  Anusha -> Owner:
    * Chapter 10 Stage-1 punishment voice recording  (private upload)
    * Chapter 5 voice recording                      (private upload)

  NOT editable from the site (fixed in the HTML, a new publish is needed to change them):
    chapter story text, quiz/riddle text, game logic, animations.

STEP 1  Owner login
  Supabase > Authentication > Users > Add user. Use your Owner email + a strong password.
  Tick "Auto Confirm User". Keep the password private.
  Then: Authentication > Sign In / Providers > turn OFF "Allow new users to sign up".

STEP 2  Run the SQL
  Open SUPABASE_SETUP.sql in a text editor. Find the line marked "THE ONE LINE YOU EDIT".
  Replace OWNER_EMAIL_HERE with your exact Owner email (keep the single quotes).
  Supabase > SQL Editor > New query > paste the WHOLE file > Run.
  If it says "No owner registered", the email does not match the user from Step 1.
  The file is safe to run again.

STEP 3  Check Storage (Supabase > Storage)
  You should see two buckets:  gallery (Public)  and  voice (Private).

STEP 4  Keys
  birthday8-owner/cloud-config.js and birthday8-visitor/cloud-config.js already contain the
  same Project URL and publishable key. Do not change them. NEVER put a secret / service_role key
  or the database password in these files.

STEP 5  Publish both folders as two separate https sites (Owner link private, Visitor link for Anusha).

STEP 6  Test (on the published Owner link, in Chrome)
  Chapter 13 > Owner Mode > sign in > "Test online sync". Every line must start with a tick.
  Then add a small photo, open the Visitor link in another browser/phone and confirm it appears.
  Anusha records in Chapter 10 -> on the Owner site open Chapter 13 > "Anusha's online voice
  recordings" > Load recordings > Load & listen.
  To publish your voice message or website song: first sign in under Chapter 13, then use the
  Owner-only setup on the first page.

PRIVACY
  * Chapter 5/10 screens now tell Anusha her recording is uploaded online for the Owner.
  * Recordings go to a PRIVATE bucket. Anusha's phone can only ADD files; only the signed-in Owner
    can list, play, download or delete them.
  * Gallery photos/videos, your voice message and the website song are readable by anyone who has
    their link (they are meant for the Visitor).
  * Anyone who views the site's source can see the public key and could upload an audio file
    (max 10 MB, audio types only) into the voice bucket. They still cannot read or delete anything.
    Check Storage > voice now and then and delete junk.
